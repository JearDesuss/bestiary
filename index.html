THE IDEA BEHIND THE BEASTS

A beautifully
strange encounter.

Bestiary is a digital cabinet of impossible creatures. Give it a name and discover a figure somewhere between animal, human, and an old painting.

Fragments from six supplied painting studies are cut and recombined in your browser. Your name chooses the head, setting, fragments, and marks. The same name always returns the same composition.

The source artwork is drawn from the supplied references, credited to jaka lodhang. The remixes are an independent experiment, not new works by the artist. The playful interaction is inspired by HEADDDS by alxxvine.

Names stay in your browser and shareable URLs. Photos are hashed on your device, never uploaded, and are not used as a likeness. Your cabinet is saved only in this browser.
